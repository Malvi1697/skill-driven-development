# Base skill template

Copy the block below into `.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`.

Fill in right away, from what you already know:

- `name` and `description`
- **Goal**, **Constraints** and **Commands**

**Steps** and **References** may keep their `SDD-TODO` while the work is in
progress. Fill them in as you learn, or remove a section that turns out to
be empty. Replace each `SDD-TODO` line whole, hint included. No `SDD-TODO`
may remain when the work is committed.

```markdown
---
name: SDD-TODO
description: SDD-TODO
---

# SDD-TODO Title naming the kind of work

## Goal

SDD-TODO What a good result of this kind of work looks like, in a sentence or two.

## Constraints

## Commands

SDD-TODO The project commands this work needs, such as run, test, lint and build.

## Steps

## References

```
